TITLE: Cryptographic Architecture Guidelines
PARENT: Code Guidelines and Standards
UPDATED: 2023-07-27
URL: https://confluence.alkami.com/spaces/SDKC/pages/282928359
-----
Alkami recommends that you adhere to encryption best practices to ensure all data is protected, both in transit and at rest. We follow NIST guidelines, particularly NIST SP 800-57. At a minimum, secure data in transit with protocols like TLS 1.2. For data at rest, use AES 256 encryption, at a minimum, to provide a high level of security. For publicly signed certificates Alkami follows the recommendations of the CA/Browser forum and limits public certificates to a maximum of 398 days. For all other certificate types, follow industry best practices.
