## Overview

Methods provide a means to compute a value once and reuse it in multiple places throughout the configuration. They can also act as a variable to help simplify complex behaviors by breaking down complex logic into multiple separate expressions. See the "Method Example" on Standard SSO (https://confluence.alkami.com/display/ISG/Standard+SSO#StandardSSO-Examples).

## Limitations
- Methods must have a name of at least one character in length.
- Method names are case-insensitive.
- Methods can leverage any expression and can refer to other methods.
- Methods cannot form cyclical trees (e.g. A refers to B which refers to A).
